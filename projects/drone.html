Custom FPV Drone Gear

1. The GPS Holder

Designed in Fusion 360, this holder is printed using TPU (flexible filament). It absorbs impact during crashes, protecting the fragile GPS chip while maintaining the optimal angle for satellite locking.

2. Goggles & Controller Box

This rigid container was printed to fit the specific dimensions of my FPV goggles and radio controller. It features internal dividers to prevent the devices from scratching each other during travel.

3. Drone Bag Strap

I designed a custom buckle and strap system that attaches externally to my backpack. It allows me to securely mount the drone to the outside of the bag, leaving more room inside for batteries and tools.
